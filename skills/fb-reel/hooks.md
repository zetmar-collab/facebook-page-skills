# Hook patterns for short video (and post openers)

A short video has two hooks that fire at once, and they are not the same
sentence: the first thing you **say** and the first thing on **screen**. The
spoken hook has about two seconds. The on-screen hook has about one, because it is
read before it is heard. Keep on-screen text to six words, with no punctuation it
does not need.

These are common patterns in short-form content, written up in our own words. They
are starting points, not formulas that guarantee anything. Test them on your own
Page and let `/fb-audit` tell you which ones work for your audience.

Rules that apply to all of them:

- No setup. The video starts at the sentence you would normally reach at second six.
- Say the number. "A lot" is not a number.
- The first frame is a hook too. A still face at 0:00 with no motion and no text
  wastes three hooks.
- If the hook would work for anyone in your niche, it is not yet *your* hook. Put
  the thing only you can say in it.
- A hook promises something. Deliver before the halfway mark.
- Numbers and results must be real. If you do not have one, write `{{your number}}`
  and flag it. Never invent.

| # | Name | Pattern | Example | On screen |
|---|------|---------|---------|-----------|
| 1 | Cost of a mistake | {Amount} is what {one mistake} cost me. | $4,000 is what one missing clause cost me. | $4,000 MISTAKE |
| 2 | Stop doing X | Stop {common habit}. Do {alternative}. | Stop keeping one chat open for a week. Start a new one per topic. | STOP THE ONE LONG CHAT |
| 3 | Nobody tells you | Nobody tells you that {uncomfortable truth}. | Nobody tells you your first 30 posts are supposed to flop. | FIRST 30 WILL FLOP |
| 4 | The replacement | {This} replaced {expensive thing} for {price}. | This $12 tool replaced a $2,000 service. | $2,000 -> $12 |
| 5 | Time collapse | This used to take me {long}. Now it takes {short}. | Proposals took five hours. Now twenty minutes. | 5 HOURS -> 20 MIN |
| 6 | Receipts | I did {thing} for {N days}. Here are the real numbers. | I posted daily for 60 days. Real numbers. | 60 DAYS. REAL NUMBERS. |
| 7 | Wrong, but not your fault | You are doing {thing} wrong, and it is not your fault. {Fix}. | You are prompting wrong, and it is not your fault. | NOT YOUR FAULT |
| 8 | Inside view | I spent {N years} {inside the thing}. Here is what we never said out loud. | Ten years in support. What we never told customers. | 10 YEARS INSIDE |
| 9 | Take this | Take this {asset}. It took me {time} to get right. | Take this checklist. It took me a month to get right. | TAKE THIS |
| 10 | If you... | If {specific situation}, this {short time} fixes it. | If your posts get views but no followers, this fixes it. | VIEWS BUT NO FOLLOWERS? |
| 11 | Numbered, with a favorite | {N} {things}. Number {k} is the one nobody uses. | 5 edits. Number 4 is the one nobody uses. | 5 EDITS. #4 IS THE ONE. |
| 12 | The objection | "{Objection people really say}." Fine. Here is the version that works anyway. | "I hate being on camera." Fine. Here is a version without your face. | "I HATE CAMERA" |
| 13 | Before and after | {Show old}. {Show new}. {One sentence on the lever}. | (two frames) | MARCH / NOW |
| 14 | Call out a group | {Specific group}, this is the one you keep ignoring. | Small shop owners: this is the one you keep ignoring. | SHOP OWNERS |
| 15 | The flop record | My first {N} {attempts} did nothing. Here is what changed on {N+1}. | My first 40 posts did nothing. Here is what changed. | 40 FLOPS, THEN THIS |
| 16 | The verbatim question | "{Question exactly as asked}" - I get this every week. | "Why does it forget what I said?" I get this every week. | ASKED EVERY WEEK |
| 17 | Head to head | {A} versus {B}. I used both for {time}; one was not close. | Tool A versus tool B. Two weeks of both. | A vs B |
| 18 | Mid-action open | {Start already doing the thing.} Watch what happens. | (screen recording already running) Watch this. | WATCH THIS |
| 19 | The deadline | {Thing} changes on {date}. Do {action} before then. | This setting changes on the 1st. Fix it before. | BEFORE THE 1ST |
| 20 | Permission | You do not need {thing everyone says you need}. | You do not need to be a programmer. | NO CODING NEEDED |
| 21 | Mid-sentence start | "...and that is when {the turn}." | "...and that is when he asked for a refund." | HE ASKED FOR A REFUND |
| 22 | Inverted saying | {A familiar saying turned around.} | Slow and steady loses the race. | (the twisted saying) |

Patterns that need a real, sourced fact (use only with one): *a statistic*
("{N}% of {group} {surprising fact}") and *someone else's result* ("one {person}
{result} in {time}"). Name the source on screen or in the caption.

Pick three patterns that genuinely fit the idea, write the spoken and on-screen
line for each, and choose by: a concrete number, readable at a glance, and
something only you can say.
